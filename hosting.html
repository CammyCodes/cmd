Hosting & Support Terms

Last updated 3 August 2026

These terms cover hosting, SSL, backups and ongoing support. They form part of our Terms & Conditions.

Read this bit if you read nothing else. Your site runs on third-party managed infrastructure, so server uptime and the physical security of the hardware sit with the hosting provider under their service level agreement, not with us personally. Backups are different — those are ours, and we manage them for you. Alongside that, we handle set-up, configuration and ongoing support.

1. What hosting includes

  • Your site deployed on established managed hosting infrastructure.
  • An SSL certificate, so the site loads over HTTPS.
  • Daily backups, managed by us — see section 3.
  • Configuration and deployment by us.
  • Free for the first year on new builds, as stated in your quote. After that, a monthly or annual fee we agree with you in advance.

2. Uptime — who is responsible

We choose reputable providers, but we do not own or operate the servers, and we do not offer an uptime guarantee of our own. Any uptime commitment is the one in the hosting provider's own service level agreement, and any remedy for failing to meet it is theirs to give. We will tell you who your provider is and point you at their SLA.

If your site goes down we will do what we reasonably can to get it back, including raising it with the provider. We are not liable for downtime, nor for loss caused by it.

We do not provide 24/7 monitoring or an emergency call-out service, and nothing in these terms should be read as promising one.

3. Backups

Backups are ours to manage, and we take responsibility for them. You do not need to arrange anything, monitor anything or hold your own copy.

Your site and its database are backed up daily. We keep recent restore points, we check the backups are running, and if a site needs restoring, that is our job to do — not something you have to arrange or chase.

If you would like your own copy of the site and database at any point — for peace of mind or for your records — just ask and we will send it. That is your right, not a condition of the service.

4. Response times

We aim to reply to support requests within one working day.

That is a target response time, not a guaranteed fix time. How long a fix takes depends on what has broken and whether it sits with us or with the hosting provider.

5. Included changes

New builds include a fair-use allowance of small changes in the first year, agreed with you in your quote.

A small change means text edits, swapping an image, updating opening hours or prices, adding a page section — the routine upkeep of a live site.

It does not mean: new pages, new features, redesigns, new integrations, or migrating to a different platform. Those are new work and are quoted separately. If a request goes beyond the allowance we will tell you before doing it, with a price — never afterwards on an invoice.

6. What is not included

  • Domain name registration and renewal — normally held in your own name, and your cost.
  • Third-party subscriptions and licences: email hosting, booking systems, stock imagery, paid plugins.
  • Stripe fees and any other payment processing costs.
  • Content writing and photography beyond what the original quote included.
  • Fixing problems caused by changes you or another supplier made to the site.
  • Search engine ranking. We build sites so search engines can read them properly; nobody can guarantee a position in search results, and we do not.

7. Ending hosting or support

Either of us can end the arrangement with 30 days' written notice. Fees already paid for the current period are not refunded.

If you leave, we will give you a full copy of your site files and database so you can move to another provider. We will not withhold your site, your content or your domain. We will keep your site online for 30 days after the arrangement ends to give you time to migrate.

We may suspend hosting where fees are more than 30 days overdue, or where a site is being used unlawfully — but not without telling you first and giving you a chance to put it right.

8. Your responsibilities

  • Keep your domain registration current. Sites go down most often because a domain quietly expired.
  • Keep account credentials secure and tell us promptly if something looks wrong.
  • Make sure content you publish is lawful and that you have the rights to it.

9. Liability

Our liability for hosting and support is limited to the hosting and support fees you paid us in the 12 months before the issue arose. Where hosting is provided free in the first year, that limit is the value of the build fee attributable to hosting.

Nothing here limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited, and if you are a consumer, nothing here affects your statutory rights.
